\begin{letter}{Professor Eyke H\"ullermeier\\Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery}
\opening{Dear Professor H\"ullermeier,}

Re: revision of manuscript \nolinkurl{1e58c388-d7ec-4084-b792-df20a48e3e75},
``Baseline-Relative Predictability Horizons for Cross-Domain Forecast Evaluation.''

We thank you and the Reviewers for a careful and constructive assessment. We enclose the clean
revised manuscript and a point-by-point response. The main changes are:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt]
\item \textbf{Definitions and use.} The relaxed horizon now states explicitly that non-positive
steps can lie inside its span, and a new subsection explains what the relaxed and strict horizons add to
fixed-horizon summaries, and what they do not establish. We discuss why using the descriptors to choose horizons
dynamically would require a prospective or nested evaluation, since otherwise it risks adaptive selection bias.
\item \textbf{Presentation.} Figures~1 and~2 were redrawn, all figures are cited, and units are stated.
\item \textbf{Alternative baseline.} We added a seasonal-persistence sensitivity on exactly matched
support for all four domains.
\item \textbf{ARIMA.} The order $(2,0,0)$ was fixed a priori and not selected with ACF or information
criteria. We say so, report an AIC-based order diagnostic and the ACF/PACF in a new appendix, and present the
check as an illustration of model-class sensitivity.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Corrections to previously reported results.} In preparing the revision we found and corrected three
reporting issues, all described in the response letter. (i)~The PM$_{2.5}$ text reported
$H^*(\mathrm{strict})=13$, which came from a moving-average experiment; the plotted LightGBM curve gives $22$, and the
text and Table~3 now follow the curve. (ii)~The wind ARIMA check had requested a one-step forecast at every horizon; the
$h$-step rerun leaves the wind descriptors unchanged. (iii)~The PM$_{2.5}$ row of the RMSE appendix was inherited
from the same moving-average experiment and was recomputed ($H^*(\mathrm{strict})=23$).

\textbf{Additional experiments.} The wind ARIMA rerun, the ARIMA order diagnostic, the RMSE recomputation (which reproduces the
reported MAE exactly) and the seasonal-persistence sensitivity used the same data and protocol as the paper; no new model
class, hyperparameter or split was introduced.

\textbf{Data.} The exact inputs of all four experiments, with SHA-256 checksums, are deposited at
\url{https://github.com/EduIntellect/paper2H/tree/data-cache-v1/release/data_cache_four_domain}. For the traffic series, whose underlying
measurements come from Caltrans PeMS, we state that we have not obtained separate confirmation of
redistribution terms and will remove the file on request.

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our work.
\closing{Sincerely,}
