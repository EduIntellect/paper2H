Positive forecast skill that appears only after an extended interval of negative skill should not be interpreted as evidence of a contiguous, practically useful predictability range. Such late-horizon recoveries can arise from baseline degradation or smoothing effects and may not reflect stable operational forecasting value. For this reason, Paper 2 reports both H*(relax) and H*(strict), with explicit h_start and h_end, to provide a robust characterization of effective predictability.
